About the School

Your Journey Towards Confident Driving Starts Here.

Shukla Car Driving School offers structured, practical driving training across two Kanpur locations. Our training fleet includes a Maruti Suzuki WagonR, Tata Tigor and Maruti Suzuki Brezza — giving learners the flexibility to choose the vehicle that suits their comfort and training goals.

We also provide simulator-based familiarisation to help new learners understand vehicle controls before moving to road training.
